## 2. Aims & Scope Summary

IJITEST publishes peer-reviewed original research papers, reviews, and technical notes covering:
1. **Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence:** Machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, cybersecurity, and cloud computing.
2. **Electronics & Communication Engineering:** Signal processing, VLSI design, embedded systems, IoT, wireless communication, and RF engineering.
3. **Electrical & Renewable Energy:** Smart grids, solar/wind systems, power electronics, and high-voltage engineering.
4. **Mechanical & Civil Engineering:** Automation, robotics, thermal engineering, structural dynamics, and environmental engineering.

## 3. Publication Ethics & COPE Compliance

* **Peer-Review Model:** Double-Blind Peer Review (author identities hidden from reviewers; reviewer identities hidden from authors).
* **Plagiarism Tolerance:** Maximum 15% similarity index (excluding bibliography and common method phrasing), screened via Similarity Check / Turnitin.
* **Licensing & Copyright:** Authors retain full copyright under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY 4.0).
* **CrossMark Policy:** Active update tracking at [https://ijitest.org/ethics](https://ijitest.org/ethics).
